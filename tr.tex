\noindent This report holds the appendices of the paper \emph{Rational Owned Choice: Finite Algebras of Multiplayer Games} \cite{GershuniOwnedChoice2026}, which gives the definitions, the notation, and the main statements. Lettered references, such as \cref{app:proofs}, are to this report. Numbered ones, such as \cref{sec:rational} and \cref{sym:thm:pairs}, are to the paper.

\paragraph{Further results.}
Several results concern structures and observations beyond the main paper's
equational classifications:
\begin{itemize}
\item Arbitrarily branching well-founded public games have finite binary
representatives, with the sharp local support bound $K-1$
(\cref{game:prop:choice-support}).
\item Linear-size reset circuits can require exponentially large equivalent
trees; canonical guards give a semantic occurrence lower bound
(\cref{reset:prop:large-circuit-table}).
\item Boolean winner observations recover the complete equilibrium value
and order of the two-player fragment (\cref{se:prop:pair-observation}).
\item A projection's singleton-profile fiber contains a copy of the entire
lower-arity core (\cref{core:thm:projection-fiber}), whereas read-once trees
have a closed profile formula for their full values and are least in their
profile fibers (\cref{core:thm:read-once-minimum}).
\item Collapsing all ambiguity gives an explicitly finitely based subvariety
with least near-unanimity arity $K+1$ (\cref{core:thm:atomic-basis}).
\item Finite powerset multiplications have a general faithful relational
representation (\cref{query:lem:powerset}); canonical quadratic bichain
extensions are definable exactly for cographs
(\cref{core:thm:cograph-extension}).
\item Exponential local interpolation can fail globally even for prime
operations whose proper identification minors and neutral residuals are
all core (\cref{core:thm:exponential-locality,core:cor:prime-obstructions}).
\end{itemize}
Verification status is recorded separately in \cref{app:verification}.
