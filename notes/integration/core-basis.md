# Complete uniform core nonfinite-basis formalization

## Status and exact scope

The required typed-cover lemma, padded-grid countermodel proposition, full
no-bounded-variable-basis theorem and hence no-finite-basis theorem are now
proved. The ambient full-native variety has the same nonfinite-basis conclusion.
The earlier K-variable native obstruction and exact K+1-variable basis-arity
lower bound remain independently available; they are not used as substitutes
for the unbounded theorem.

All final results assume `{Role : Type u} [DecidableEq Role] [Finite Role]`
and an embedding `three : Fin 3 ↪ Role`. There is no global K>=3 assumption in
the definitions, no Fintype enumeration of Role required by the final theorem,
and no GameTheory or complexity dependency in this import closure.

The complete proposed Paper statements, including every semantic hypothesis,
are compiled in `tests/CoreBasisGrid.lean` as `core_nonfinite_basis` and
`ambient_nonfinite_basis`. Paper must repeat those propositions, not export a
named proposition alias. `Grid.grid_countermodel` in
`KFOL/Core/GridCountermodel.lean` similarly states the complete report grid
claim with an existential algebra and concrete finite-arity identity.

## Actual finite countermodels and zero variables

The final witness is B_d=Option(C_{r,r}) with r=2^d+2. C is the subalgebra of
the explicit finite product of proper native omission algebras generated by
all padded source columns and the all-lower tuple. Its actual generated
carrier is finite by finite-factor / finite-coordinate / subtype finiteness;
no enumeration of terms or finite search certificate is used.

The slightly larger r than the report's optimized sufficient bound makes one
cover work whether or not the chosen generators include the new point. For
arbitrary X in B_d, its old values form X.eraseNone. Their count is <=|X|,
and the local upper clone of <e,X.eraseNone> covers every member of X by an
actual homomorphism. The whole generated subalgebra therefore lies in that
homomorphic range and belongs to the core variety.

The theorem includes d=0. The local conclusion is stated for nonempty
closures, following the independently defined nonempty-algebra variety
convention. This is the only nonemptiness qualification; it is not a
restriction to nonempty generator sets. Identity transfer already proves
that padded arities and repeated variables depend on actual support.card.

## Proof architecture and audit surface

`Definitions/CoreBasis.lean` retains the independent native table, profile,
derived join, rigid upper clone and separating-exhaustion syntax. Shared
`Definitions/Equational.lean` supplies semantic satisfaction and actual
support. The final paper propositions mention these semantics and existential
algebras, not the grid construction's names or proof invariants.

The grid construction is explicit in `Core/GridData.lean`; source terms are
exhaustive by `GridRows.exists_source_term`. `Protected`, `GridSeparation`,
`GridPrivate`, `GridIdeal` and `GridOrder` prove all three protected-path
arguments and the only-three-pattern ideal classification for arbitrary terms.
`Folds`, `GridTerms`, `GridFoldValues`, `GridPatterns` and `GridAlgebra` prove
that the two nontrivial patterns are actual padded term values and establish
their complete required products. `GridCrossing`, `GridClone` and
`GridCountermodel` prove native validity and actual upper-clone failure.

`GridProfiles` proves the block-bit homomorphism laws on arbitrary grid
elements and the annotation guards. `PairLabel`, `MarkerGap`, `GridGuards`,
`GridIdealLabel` and `GridLocalCover` construct an actual graph-generated
subalgebra P, prove its closure invariant and exact ideal compatibility,
build its onto first projection, and apply the proved typed cover.
`GridPigeonhole` proves the exponential profile-count argument for arbitrary
generators. `UpperMap` and `HomRange` prove the final transport through actual
homomorphic ranges; the latter is a generic equational preservation lemma.

`NativeTheory.winner_equation_of_native` proves the required ambient theory
inclusion using native omission quotients that independently detect every
winner-membership bit. No reverse variety inclusion is assumed.

The generic semilattice laws now reuse the shared
`WinnerSet.choice_assoc`; the former duplicate private associativity proof in
ModelLaws was removed. `Core.lean` is consolidated to import the final
NonfiniteBasis module, whose dependencies contain this entire pure slice.
