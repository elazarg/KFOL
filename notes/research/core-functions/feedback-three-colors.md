# Strong feedback over the original owners is insufficient

Written mathematical proof, not yet formalized in Lean. An explicit
loopless three-owner closure tuple satisfies full strong feedback elimination
but has no joint pure-port realizer. A fresh identity observer exposes a
six-query feedback obstruction. The extended tuple still passes hereditary
profile compatibility at every observer-independent base and simultaneous
cross-value composition. Thus strong feedback at the original owners is
insufficient, and conditional profile tests cannot replace fresh-owner
feedback. Even fresh-stable feedback is insufficient, as the
[five-variable terminal obstruction](feedback-profile-obstruction.md) proves.

## The eight variables and six capture rules

Use colors A,B,C. Think of the six query labels as directed arcs of the
complete directed triangle, with the arc's target as its owner. Label them

    A0=B->A, A1=C->A;
    B0=A->B, B1=C->B;
    C0=A->C, C1=B->C.

The outneighbors of a query are the two arcs leaving its owner:

    A0,A1 -> B0,C0;
    B0,B1 -> A0,C1;
    C0,C1 -> A1,B1.                                  (1)

Each variable is a nonempty set of query labels. Retain exactly these eight:

    uA={A0,A1}, uB={B0,B1}, uC={C0,C1};
    p={A0,B0}, q={A1,C0}, e={B1,C1};
    t+={A0,B1,C0}, t-={A1,B0,C1}.                     (2)

They are the three incoming stars, the three directed 2-cycles, and the two
directed 3-cycles. Let V be this eight-element variable set. For a query i,
let X_i be the variables containing i, and let

    T_s=UNION_(j an outneighbor of i) X_j, owner(i)=s;
    Q_i=T_s\X_i.

The two queries of one color have the same T_s. Define d_s as the closure
generated by its two implications Q_i=>X_i. Explicitly:

| Owner/query | Premise Q_i | Captured block X_i |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | uB,uC,q,t- | uA,p,t+ |
| A1 | uB,uC,p,t+ | uA,q,t- |
| B0 | uA,uC,e,t+ | uB,p,t- |
| B1 | uA,uC,p,t- | uB,e,t+ |
| C0 | uA,uB,e,t- | uC,q,t+ |
| C1 | uA,uB,q,t+ | uC,e,t- |

Every premise has four variables and is disjoint from its head block.
The closures are loopless. If one rule of color s fires, the other fires
as well: Q_j\X_i subseteq Q_i for the two same-color queries i,j.
Consequently, writing H_s=X_(s0) union X_(s1),

    d_s(E)=E if neither Q_(s0) nor Q_(s1) is contained in E;
           E union H_s otherwise.                     (3)

In this example H_s subseteq Q_i union X_i, so a first firing adds at most
the three variables of X_i. Also Q_i union X_i is V minus the 2-cycle
variable between the other two colors. The permutation group on A,B,C
acts on the directed-arc description and preserves the entire construction;
in particular it is transitive on the six queries.

## Hereditary common-source profiles

**Theorem.** For every nonempty retained L subseteq V, if coalitions
E_A,E_B,E_C subseteq L satisfy L subseteq d_s(E_s) for each s, their
intersection is nonempty.

Call s active when one of its two rules fires from E_s. If no color is
active, each E_s=L and there is nothing to prove. If the active colors form
a proper nonempty subset, choose an inactive color t. The variable u_t
belongs to every premise of every active color, so it belongs to every
active E_s and hence to L. For an inactive color d_t(E_t)=E_t, so E_t=L.
Thus u_t is common to all three coalitions.

Suppose all three colors are active. For each s choose the first-fired
query i_s, so Q_(i_s) subseteq E_s. Delete these three query labels from
the directed triangle. Exactly one incoming arc at each vertex remains;
the remaining arcs therefore contain a directed cycle. Its length is two
or three, so its arc set Z is one of the five cycle variables in (2).

For each color s on this cycle, Z contains an outgoing arc from s and
does not contain the deleted incoming arc i_s. Hence Z belongs to
T_s\X_(i_s)=Q_(i_s), and thus to E_s. In particular Z belongs to L.
For any color outside the cycle, Z is outside H_s. Equation (3) and
L subseteq d_s(E_s) then force Z into E_s as well. This gives a common
variable in every case and proves the theorem.

## Feedback already occurs

Use all six queries, with their own colors. Their head blocks are nonempty,
their union is V, and X_i subseteq d_(owner(i))(Q_i). By construction Q_i
is covered by the two outneighbor head blocks in (1); their owners are
distinct and both differ from owner(i). Thus this is a rainbow feedback
configuration despite the hereditary profile theorem above.

For an owner set containing four distinct owners rho,A,B,C, put
c_rho=id and c_A=d_A,c_B=d_B,c_C=d_C; all additional owners are identities.
Apply strong (FE) with observing owner rho, base V, and the six queries.
All their sets lie in c_rho(V)=V, but eliminating their head union would
assert V=c_rho(empty)=empty. Therefore this closure tuple cannot be the
joint pure-port tuple of any core word.

This refutes the local aggregation implication with three foreign colors.
It does not contradict the two-foreign-color compression theorem.

## Every observer-independent-base profile test still passes

**Theorem.** For the three-owner tuple, or any extension of it by identity
owners, take any observer r and any r-independent B subseteq V.
Set D=c_r(B)\B. The conditional foreign
closures

    delta_s(E)=c_s(E union D) intersect B, s!=r,         (4)

have common-source spanning profiles on every nonempty retained subset
of B. The tuple also satisfies

    P subseteq c_r(E)\E implies c_s(P) subseteq c_r(E)  (5)

for every r,s,E,P.

If r is an identity owner, D=empty. The foreign tuple consists of the three
d_s and possibly identities; the preceding hereditary theorem applies,
since a spanning coalition of an identity closure is the entire retained
set. If r is nonidentity but inactive at B, D is again empty. The pairwise
hereditary condition for the other two d_s follows from the triple condition
by taking the omitted color's coalition to be the entire retained set.

It remains to consider an active r-independent B. If Q_i subseteq B for
an r-owned query i, every variable outside Q_i except the opposite
2-cycle variable would be redundant in B: deleting it preserves Q_i,
whose closure already contains that variable. Hence B is Q_i or Q_i
plus the opposite 2-cycle variable. Color symmetry reduces all these
cases to i=A0. Write b=uB, c=uC, v=t-. Then

    B-={b,c,q,v}, B+=B- union {e}, D={uA,p,t+}.

For B-, the two nonidentity foreign closures have just the rules

    delta_B: {c,v}=>b;
    delta_C: {b,q}=>{c,v}.                            (6)

The variable q is uncaptured by both. If a retained set contains q, every
spanning coalition contains it. Otherwise delta_C is the identity on that
retained set, and looplessness of delta_B gives a nonempty intersection.

For B+, the conditional rules are

    delta_B: {c,e}=>{b,v}, {c,v}=>{b,e};
    delta_C: {b,e,v}=>{c,q}, {b,q}=>{c,e,v}.           (7)

Suppose two disjoint coalitions span a nonempty retained set. Both closures
must activate, since an inactive one requires its coalition to equal the
retained set, and neither closure has loops. The first activation of delta_C
cannot use {b,q}: q is uncaptured by delta_B, so its presence in the
delta_C coalition would force it into the other spanning coalition too.
Thus the delta_C coalition contains both e and v. But delta_B requires at
least one of e,v to activate. This contradicts disjointness and proves
the remaining profile tests. Extra identity foreign owners have no effect.

Finally every nonidentity closure adds at most three new variables by (3).
Thus a set P as in (5) has size at most three, and cannot contain any
four-variable premise. Every c_s fixes P, proving (5). Identity observers
have P=empty and satisfy the same conclusion.

Accordingly, even the conditional hereditary profile tests at all observers
and independent bases, together with simultaneous cross-value composition,
do not imply strong (FE). Repeated-color feedback retains information that
these profile tests miss.

## The three-owner tuple satisfies full strong feedback

**Theorem.** With exactly the three owners A,B,C, the tuple d_A,d_B,d_C
satisfies strong (FE), but no core word jointly realizes its pure ports.

For each observing owner, exactly two foreign colors remain. The preceding
theorem gives hereditary common-source compatibility of their conditional
closures at every independent base. The
[two-color compression theorem](three-owner-exchange.md#two-color-compression),
together with the independent-base equivalence, therefore gives full strong
(FE) for this three-owner tuple, including configurations with arbitrarily
many repeated-color queries.

Suppose a core word T realized the tuple. Interpret the same syntax after
adjoining a fresh owner rho. Its old-owner traces are unchanged: each still
replaces its own gates by multiplication and all foreign gates by join.
Looplessness forces T to use all eight variables. Since T has no rho-owned
gate, its rho trace is their full union, with pure closure id. Thus T would
jointly realize the four-owner extension considered above. Its six-query
feedback violation gives a contradiction.

This uses ambient-owner independence of pure traces, not preservation of
the entire winner-valued operation or its source-separable reconstruction.
The obstruction excludes every joint pure-port realizer, including words
whose full source coefficients do not separate by owner.

## Unused-owner invariance and one fresh feedback observer

For K>=3, a loopless full-support tuple c is jointly realizable on I if and
only if (c,id_rho) is jointly realizable on I union {rho}. The forward
direction is the trace argument above. Conversely, in a realizer over the
extended owner set replace every rho-owned gate by the old derived union.
At every old coordinate the removed gate and its replacement both act as
join, so all old pure traces are preserved. Thus unused-owner extension
does not alter the joint pure-port problem.

Consequently a necessary condition is strong (FE) after adjoining one fresh
identity owner rho. This one extension also captures every (FE) test
involving any larger finite set of unused owners.

Indeed an identity-colored query satisfies X subseteq id(P)=P and
X intersect P=empty, so X=empty. Such queries add no heads, contribute nothing
to any rainbow cover, and can be removed without changing a feedback failure.
For an old observing owner, every failure therefore already uses only old
query colors. For an unused observing owner, remove all other unused query
colors and rename that observer rho. The same failure occurs in the
one-identity extension. Conversely its tests are included among the larger
extensions' tests.

Hence feedback stability under arbitrary unused-owner extensions is exactly
feedback compatibility with one fresh identity coordinate. The example passes
all original-owner feedback laws and fails this necessary strengthened test.
The [five-variable terminal obstruction](feedback-profile-obstruction.md) passes
this strengthened condition and still has no common pure-port realizer.
