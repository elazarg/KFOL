\section{Conclusion}

Possible pure SPE winners give a concrete semantics for owned choice.
Its equations have optimal singleton-and-pair tests, and its idempotent
operations identify a smaller syntax intrinsically. That core retains
the canonical player views while already having coNP-complete equation
validity. Its binary atom-preserving part consists exactly of reset
and the projections.

Reset connects this semantics to an established finitely based order
algebra. Prerequisite tables and antimatroids describe all its term
functions, mixed valuations, and substitution. Its conservative models
and explicit combs exhibit the known residual largeness. The larger
algebra retains owner-sensitive operations that reset cannot express;
the unary and congruence classifications mark further exact boundaries.

The remaining logical question is a uniform presentation of the larger
core. The remaining type-theoretic question is how connection preserves
stable witnesses, full strategies, and permitted observations together.
The equations, counterexamples, and formal interfaces here give concrete
requirements against which those constructions can be tested.
