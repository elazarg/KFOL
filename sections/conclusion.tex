\section{Conclusion}

Possible pure SPE winners give a concrete semantics for owned choice, and a small one. Its equations have optimal
singleton-and-pair tests, and its idempotent operations identify a smaller syntax intrinsically. That core retains the
canonical player views, yet its equations are already coNP-complete. Its binary atom-preserving part consists exactly of
reset and the projections.

The boundary of tractability is exact. Owned choice, union, and reset each have polynomial equality on their own, and every
pair of them is hard. One owner's guarded choice keeps polynomial equality, decided by a single outsider's guard table, with a
common four-element generator for every $K\geq3$, while two such owners are hard. For an individual observer the threshold is two
owners other than the observer, so that the two owners of a hard fragment can each have easy observations while an outsider has a
hard one. Complete-graph identities show that the number of roles becomes visible again once union is added to reset.

Reset connects this semantics to an established finitely based order algebra. Prerequisite tables and antimatroids describe all its
term functions, on mixed valuations and under substitution, and its conservative models and explicit combs exhibit the known residual
largeness. The larger algebra retains owner-sensitive operations that reset cannot express, and the unary and congruence classifications
mark further exact boundaries.

Beyond public choice the values must become quantitative. For complete-revelation rounds, mixed SPE distributions, equal to serial SE values,
compose by a Nash continuation operator, and a two-player scalar fragment realizes parallel sum and all rational win probabilities. Three-player
contexts distinguish infinitely many Boolean sealed games with identical possible winners. So the finite winner-set algebra has an exact public
scope, and fully revealed rounds supply an infinite quantitative extension.

Two questions remain. The logical one is a uniform presentation of the larger core. The type-theoretic one is how connection preserves stable
witnesses, full strategies, and permitted observations together: full public disclosure supplies one composition boundary, and pending private
information requires a richer interface. The equations, counterexamples, and formal interfaces given here are concrete requirements against which
such constructions can be tested.
