\section{Verification details}\label{app:verification}

The formal development in \texttt{lean/} uses Lean 4.34.1 with only \texttt{Std}. It has no admitted proofs or added axioms; audits report only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound} where needed. \Cref{tab:status} records the evidence for each main result: ``Lean'' names kernel-checked theorems, ``written'' means a mathematical proof in this paper, and ``external'' means a theorem imported from the literature, which is not a Lean axiom. Finite checks test their implementations at stated sizes. Run \texttt{make check paper} to reproduce them.

\subsection{Written proofs and finite checks}

Optimal diagonal tests, complete congruence classifications,
the external $S_2$ step, unary fibers,
antimatroid representation and synthesis, forest membership in the reset
variety, and full complexity reduction have written proofs here. Finite
checks do not make them generic Lean theorems. Reset basis completeness
is imported mathematically from Freese et al., not as a Lean axiom.
Dependent script metatheory and hiding also have written proofs.
For sealed rounds, the SE correspondence, consistency and public-boundary
pasting, semialgebraic closure, scalar game realization and contextual
full abstraction have written proofs in \cref{app:proofs-sealed}.
\texttt{SealedRounds.lean} proves the stage and arithmetic certificates;
it does not define a general SE assessment or connect the full round
syntax to the threshold probes internally.
For the smaller hard fragments, \texttt{Fragments.lean} checks the
semantic certificates under the stated baseline-containment hypotheses,
including both absorption and fresh-marker separation. Assembly of
the syntactic reductions, their polynomial tree sizes, and the final
complexity statements are written proofs in \cref{app:proofs-hard}.
\texttt{RawFragments.lean} additionally checks the semantic fold,
absorption, and guard-transfer certificates for the reductions of the pair dichotomy;
it does not formalize the CNF syntax assembly or polynomial reductions.
For the tractable fragment, \texttt{OwnerFragments.lean} proves the
generic guard criterion and executable checker correctness, including
\path{Term.single_table_equation_iff}, \path{Term.equationB_correct},
and, in \texttt{OwnerModels.lean}, \path{Term.four_element_complete}.
The minimum-generator proof uses Freese et al.'s three-element
subalgebra theorem externally; its remaining ternary obstruction is
kernel-checked as \path{three_state_obstruction}. Polynomial size bounds and the
observer-relative complexity dichotomy are written deductions in
\cref{app:proofs-owner}.
The coloring proof has a generic Lean pigeonhole/guard certificate;
the complete identity family and its role-set embeddings are proved
mathematically there.
